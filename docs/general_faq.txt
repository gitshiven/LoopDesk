NOVAPAY GENERAL FAQ

1. SUPPORT HOURS
NovaPay support is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm GMT. Emergency technical support is available 24/7 for Enterprise customers.

2. ACCOUNT DELETION
Account deletion requests take 30 days to process. All data is permanently deleted after 30 days. Deletion can be requested in Settings > Account > Delete Account.

3. PASSWORD RESET
Password resets are done via email only. Click Forgot Password on the login page. Reset links expire after 1 hour.

4. TWO-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION
2FA is available via authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy) or SMS. 2FA can be enabled in Settings > Security.

5. DATA EXPORT
Customers can export all their data in CSV or JSON format from Settings > Account > Export Data. Exports are ready within 24 hours.

6. CONTACT
General enquiries: hello@novapay.io
Billing: billing@novapay.io
Technical: tech@novapay.io

7. TWO-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION SETUP
To enable two-factor authentication on your NovaPay account go to Settings then Security. Choose your preferred 2FA method: authenticator app (Google Authenticator or Authy) or SMS. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete setup. 2FA is available to all NovaPay accounts.

8. DATA EXPORT
To export your data go to Settings then Account then Export Data. Choose your preferred format: CSV or JSON. Your export will be ready within 24 hours and you will receive an email notification when it is ready to download.
