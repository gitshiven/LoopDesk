NOVAPAY GENERAL FAQ

1. SUPPORT HOURS
NovaPay support is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm GMT. Emergency technical support is available 24/7 for Enterprise customers.

2. ACCOUNT DELETION
Account deletion requests take 30 days to process. All data is permanently deleted after 30 days. Deletion can be requested in Settings > Account > Delete Account.

3. PASSWORD RESET
Password resets are done via email only. Click Forgot Password on the login page. Reset links expire after 1 hour.

4. TWO-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION
2FA is available via authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy) or SMS. 2FA can be enabled in Settings > Security.

5. DATA EXPORT
Customers can export all their data in CSV or JSON format from Settings > Account > Export Data. Exports are ready within 24 hours.
