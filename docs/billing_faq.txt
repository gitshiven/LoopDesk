NOVAPAY BILLING FAQ

1. DOUBLE CHARGES
If a customer is charged twice for the same transaction, a full refund is issued automatically within 3-5 business days. Customers should email billing@novapay.io with their transaction ID.

2. REFUND POLICY
Refunds are eligible if requested within 30 days of the original charge. Crypto payments are non-refundable. Refunds are returned to the original payment method only.

3. FAILED PAYMENTS
Failed payments are retried automatically 3 times over 7 days. If all retries fail, the account is suspended. Customers can update their payment method in Settings > Billing.

4. SUBSCRIPTIONS
Subscriptions renew on the 1st of each month. Cancellations must be made 48 hours before renewal to avoid being charged for the next cycle.

5. INVOICES
Invoices are emailed automatically on the day of each charge. Past invoices are available in Settings > Billing > Invoice History.

6. VAT
VAT is applied to all EU customers at the applicable local rate. VAT numbers can be added in Settings > Billing > Tax Information.

7. SUBSCRIPTION CANCELLATION
To cancel your subscription go to Settings then Billing. Click Cancel Subscription. Cancellations must be made at least 48 hours before your next renewal date to avoid being charged for the next cycle. Subscriptions renew on the 1st of each month.

8. UPDATING PAYMENT METHOD
To update your payment method go to Settings then Billing then Payment Methods. Add a new card or bank account and set it as the default. Changes take effect immediately for future charges.
